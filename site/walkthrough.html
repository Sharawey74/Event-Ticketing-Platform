Product Walkthrough

A real journey, captured live on production.

Every screenshot below is the actual live deployment — real seeded Egyptian events, a real Stripe test-mode purchase, and a real organizer account. Click any image to zoom.

Discover

Landing page

The real photo-backed hero with a functional search bar (query, city, date), a trust-stat row, and a featured-events strip backed by real published event data — not placeholder cards.

Search & filter

Filtering by category, city, text, and date against real seeded Egyptian venues and events — Cairo, Alexandria, Hurghada, Sharm El Sheikh, Dahab, and El Gouna.

Event detail

Full venue, schedule, and organizer info, with live ticket tiers pulled straight from PostgreSQL — availableCount decremented atomically on every reservation.

Book

Ticket selection

Tier selection with live quantity controls and a running total, calculated client-side and re-verified server-side at reservation time.

Stripe checkout (test mode)

A genuine Stripe-hosted checkout session — not a mock. Filled here with Stripe's own test card (4242 4242 4242 4242) to prove the integration is real, end to end.

Payment confirmation

Stripe's own redirect confirms the charge succeeded immediately after payment.

Manage

Attendee dashboard

Booking history with live countdown timers on held reservations, and one-click resume for an in-progress checkout.

Booking detail & QR ticket

Once a webhook confirms payment, this page renders the ticket's QR code for check-in.

The mid-processing state shown in the confirmation screenshot above led to root-causing two real bugs: a lazy-loading crash on reservation, and a webhook whose confirmation could be rolled back by a messaging hiccup. Both are fixed; this ticket reflects the corrected flow.

Organizer dashboard

Sales-over-time chart and per-event sales progress, revenue, and quick actions — logged in as a real seeded organizer account.

Attendee check-in

The organizer-side attendee list, searchable by name, email, or booking reference — this is where day-of check-in happens.

Responsive

Mobile layout

The same landing page at a 390px mobile viewport — single-column stack, full-width cards, no horizontal scroll.
